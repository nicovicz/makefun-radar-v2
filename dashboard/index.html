TRANSPARENT MODEL / ZERO HARDCODED RANKS

How Meridian derives implied value

Every rank is computed directly from the sealed snapshot: TVL × Shrunk Multiple × Momentum × Maturity × Breadth. Scenarios in the detail drawer never alter the ranked board.

01 · Shrunk Peer Multiple

Clamped peer Mcap/TVL (0.05×–12×) shrunk toward the global median with a 3-peer Bayesian prior so thin 1-peer categories never distort ranks.

02 · 7d TVL Momentum

1 + clamp(change_7d, -50%, +50%) × 0.50 / 100. Missing 7d change defaults neutrally to 0%.

03 · Operating Maturity

0.75 + 0.25 × min(age_days, 730) / 730. Rewards battle-tested protocols running without a token.

04 · Multi-Chain Breadth

1 + 0.05 × min(chains - 1, 4). Modest premium for multi-network liquidity distribution.
